About Me

I'm a BSIT student at Bulacan State University who enjoys turning everyday problems into working web apps. My favorite projects are the ones people can actually use, like a campus navigation tool for students and a market-replay simulator for learning how to invest.

I work mostly with TypeScript, React, and Node.js, and I care about clean code and layouts that work on every screen, from a small phone to a wide desktop monitor.

Like Frieren, I believe the journey matters as much as the destination, so every project is a chance to learn a new spell.
